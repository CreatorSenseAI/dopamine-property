Privacy policy

Auction Empires · last updated 24 October 2026

Auction Empires is a free property-auction game made by CreatorSense AI (Sydney, Australia). This page explains what the game stores and why. The short version: your game is saved to an account so you can't lose it; your email and a password hash are only stored when you protect your game; nothing is sold.

What the game stores on your device

Your game (homes, cash, level, settings) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device, and the native app keeps the same save in its own storage.

What is sent to our server

  • Anonymous play statistics: a random install ID (not linked to you), the game version, your device's screen size and browser type, and what happened in the game (auctions joined, wins, levels, purchases made through the app stores). We use this to balance the game and fix bugs. You can turn it off in the menu under Anonymous play stats.
  • Your nickname, if you set one, so it can appear on leaderboards, in friend groups and in live auctions. Pick anything you like; it does not need to be your real name.
  • Your account and cloud save: the first time the game opens online it creates an account (a random ID, no personal details) and keeps a copy of your save on our server, updated as you play, so a cleared browser or a lost phone doesn't lose your game.
  • Your email and password, when you protect your game: the game asks you to add an email and a password so you can sign in on any device. Your email is used to sign you in and, if you forget your password, to send you a 6-digit code (through our email provider, Resend). We send no marketing to it. Your password is never stored: we keep only a salted one-way hash of it (scrypt), which can't be turned back into the password. Players who set a username earlier can keep signing in with it.
  • Transfer codes: when you ask to move your game to another device, your save is stored on our server under a code for 30 days, then deleted.
  • Feedback you send from the menu (your star rating and comment).
  • Push notification tokens (app only, if you allow notifications) so we can tell you about your daily lot or a streak about to end. Turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

What we do not collect

No phone number, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers or payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple and Google; we only receive a receipt that says which item was bought.

Cookies and analytics

The web game uses no advertising cookies. If web analytics are enabled, they are privacy-respecting page-view counts without cookies.

Children

The game has an optional R18+ mode with darker humour that is off by default and asks you to confirm you are an adult. Accounts are not intended for children under 13.

Where data lives and how long

Play statistics, accounts and cloud saves are stored on servers and a database hosted by Railway in the United States and kept for up to 12 months. Transfer codes expire after 30 days. Your account and cloud save are kept while you play, and deleted when you delete your account. Leaderboard entries are kept while the board is live.

Your choices

Turn off play statistics in the menu, change or remove your nickname, delete your account and its cloud save in Settings, then Your account, clear your browser's site data to delete the save on your device, or email info@creatorsenseai.com to ask what we hold under your transfer code or nickname and have it removed.
