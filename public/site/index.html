Questions

Anything else, email info@creatorsenseai.com.

Is Auction Empires free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser right now. The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon, free to download with optional in-app purchases.

Is there an app?

The iPhone and Android apps are on the way. Until then it plays in Safari or Chrome on your phone, and you can add it to your home screen. When the apps land, you'll be able to bring your game across.

Is it real money?

No. It's a game. There's no betting, and game cash can't be cashed out or traded.

Are the prices real?

The suburbs and the auction-day rituals are real. The prices, rents and rivals are game numbers, so please don't take it to your mortgage broker.

How long does a game take?

An auction lasts ten to fifteen seconds and a game month passes every second, so a two-minute break is enough to win a home and watch the rent come in.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Start a group and share the code for a weekly league, duel on the same lot, meet on Thursday nights for Auction Night, or jump into the live arena where everyone online bids on one trophy home.

Does my game save?

Yes. Your empire saves to your account as you play. Add an email and a password in Settings and you can sign in on any phone, tablet or computer and carry on.
