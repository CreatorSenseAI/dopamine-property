Questions

Anything else, email info@creatorsenseai.com.

Is Auction Empires free?

Yes. The whole game is free in your phone or computer browser. The app has optional purchases such as cash packs and Pro, and none of them win an auction for you.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the game in Safari or Chrome and start bidding. You can add it to your home screen to play it like an app.

Is it gambling or real money?

No. Game cash never turns back into money, there is no betting and nothing for sale is a chance-based reward. You win homes by bidding better than the rivals.

Are the prices real?

The suburbs and the auction rituals are real; the prices, rents and rivals are game numbers tuned to be fast and fair, so it is a game, not property advice.

How long does a game take?

Each auction runs ten to fifteen seconds and a game month passes every second, so a two-minute break is enough to win a home and see the rent come in.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Start a group and share its code for a weekly league, duel on the same lot, meet on Thursday nights for Auction Night, or join the live arena where everyone online bids on one property.

Does my game save?

Your empire saves on your device automatically. To move it to another phone or into the app, make a transfer code from Settings.
